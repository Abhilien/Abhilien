The Production World

A working test of one idea. The world exists first, and events actually happen in it. Every shot is just a camera looking at that world, so cameras can change without the story changing. One room, two characters, ten objects, ten steps.

Scale is part of the world

Sizes and ratios

Every size is stored in metres once, in the master world. Shots never resize anything. Change it here and every step and every camera uses the new size.

Ratios held by the world

With proportions locked, changing one height rescales the whole world, so a doll's-house version of the scene looks exactly like the full-size one. Unlock it to make a deliberate story choice, such as a giant in a normal room, and watch the ratios change.
